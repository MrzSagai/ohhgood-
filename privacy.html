THE LITTLE DETAILS

Privacy Policy.

Effective date: October 24, 2026. This notice describes Ohhgood!'s current website setup and should be reviewed again before public launch or whenever services change.

What we collect

Ohhgood! currently does not offer account registration, an on-site checkout, a newsletter signup, or an on-site contact form. We do not request payment card information or maintain a customer order database on this website.

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP addresses, browser and device details, requests, and security logs. We do not claim that visiting the site is completely anonymous.

External websites and affiliate links

Some product links lead to Amazon or other independent retailers. Clicking them can allow those services to process information according to their own privacy and cookie policies. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Purchases, checkout, returns, and payment processing happen on the retailer's website, not Ohhgood!.

Cookies and analytics

Ohhgood! currently stores your selected light or dark theme in your browser's local storage. We have not intentionally enabled a site-owned analytics or advertising-tracking script in this version. Hosting and third-party services, including externally loaded fonts and article images, may process browser requests; their practices are governed by their policies.

If we add analytics, advertising pixels, forms, or newsletters later, this notice and any legally required notices or consent controls will be reviewed before enabling them.

Third-party content

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and editorial imagery from Unsplash. Their servers may receive technical request information when those assets load.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to solicit information from children.

Questions and changes

This policy may be updated to reflect service and operational changes. Contact instructions will be published on our Contact page once the public support channel is confirmed.
