THE LITTLE DETAILS

Contact.

Have a question about an Ohhgood! recommendation, a product detail, or our editorial approach? We want to make it easy to reach the team.

General inquiries

Email us at hello@ohhgood.store. We read every message and aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include the product name or page URL you are asking about so we can help faster.

Retailer orders

Ohhgood! does not sell or ship products directly. All purchases happen on the retailer's website. For order status, payments, cancellations, returns, and refunds, please contact the retailer where you made your purchase (e.g., Amazon). If you bought via Amazon, check your Amazon account > Your Orders.

Product feedback & corrections

If you spot an error in a product detail, a broken retailer link, or an image that doesn't match the listed product, let us know at hello@ohhgood.store with subject "Correction". We verify retailer links regularly and aim to fix issues promptly.

Press & partnerships

For press or partnership inquiries, email hello@ohhgood.store with subject "Partnership".
