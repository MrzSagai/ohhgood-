How it actually works on a real desk

There are two parts: a small magnetic clip that wraps around your cable, and a flat magnetic base that sticks to your desk or nightstand. The clip clicks to the base with a magnet, so the cable hangs in place. When you need it, you just pull, no unclipping, no sticky residue to fight with.

  1. Pick your spot: desk edge, side of your monitor stand, or bedside table. Flat and clean is best.
  2. Stick the base: peel and stick it, press for 10 seconds, let it sit for a few minutes.
  3. Clip your cable: snap the clip around the cable near the connector, then let it magnet to the base.
  4. Test the pull: tug gently. It should come off with one hand, but not slide on its own.

For most people, 2 to 3 clips cover the daily rotation: phone charger, laptop charger, and earbuds cable. The rest of the 9-pack is good for a second spot like the nightstand or a travel pouch.

FAQ

Will it leave marks if I remove it?

On smooth desks, usually no, it is adhesive, not a screw. Warm it with a hair dryer for 10 seconds before peeling to make removal cleaner. Always test a hidden spot first.

Can I use it on a wall?

Yes, if the wall is smooth. On painted drywall, check that the paint is fully cured. For renters, desk edge is safer than wall.

Does it work with MagSafe and USB-C?

Yes, the clip is about the cable, not the connector. If your cable fits in the clip, it will hold.
