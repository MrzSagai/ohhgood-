## Product image acceptance
- Use an actual photograph of the **same item, color, and pack quantity**, or an independently created original asset whose model depiction has been checked against the manufacturer's reference.
- Ensure explicit rights for using the image on Ohhgood!; a publicly displayed retailer image is not automatically licensed for republication.
- No large promotional text embedded inside product photos; no misleading details added by AI.
- Desktop and mobile Quick Look image must depict the same product. Recompress assets to improve page performance.
- Never show an affiliate button for draft entries. Do not publish until destination leads to the correct US variation.

## Site final QA — outstanding
- Test real mobile devices and desktop: category navigation, Quick Look modal focus and close, CTA placement, dark mode, no horizontal overflow.
- Replace temporary lifestyle images with approved, appropriately licensed imagery; confirm category 'On the Go' image matches theme.
- Check Journal article is real English editorial content; remove Indonesian test text before release.
- Review About, privacy policy and affiliate disclosure against actual operational practices and any deployed analytics.
- Brand lock still needs master logo and mascot SVG fidelity check; black wordmark readable in dark mode.
- Only after launch approval: revisit robots.txt and noindex rules, connect public domain and set SEO metadata.
- Future video, Instagram and Pinterest integrations are explicitly **out of scope** until launch preparation is stable.

## CMS workflow
Use **development** branch for draft review and copy. Production `main` should be merged only after product media, retailer destinations and pages pass the checklist. Preview modes are not confidential: the raw JSON file can still be fetched by anyone with a deployment URL.
