What data we collect

Ohhgood! does not offer user accounts, checkout, or newsletter signup at this time. We do not intentionally collect your name, email, or payment information through the site.

  • Contact emails: If you email us at hello@ohhgood.store, we retain your email and message to respond.

Cookies

Ohhgood! itself does not set advertising cookies. However, retailers we link to (including Amazon) may set cookies when you click a retailer link and visit their site to track referrals. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Some product imagery is provided by retailers or is illustrative. We aim to identify and replace conceptual images before items are recommended publicly. We use only images we have rights to use. Journal articles are editorial content and may be revised over time.
